Dear Editor,

Please find enclosed our manuscript "On the Validity of Metamorphic Relations for Knowledge-Conflict
Testing of Retrieval-Augmented Generation Systems", which we submit for consideration in [journal].

Metamorphic testing has recently been applied to retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) systems whose
document collections change over time. In the framework presented at ASE 2026, the operator that
injects a counterfactual fact produces the highest violation rates. Our manuscript asks whether this
kind of relation can be read as a test oracle. We propose four validity criteria for relations over
conflicting knowledge and use them to examine that framework, tracing each finding to the released
code. In a pre-registered re-analysis of the authors' released outputs, at least 77% of the reported
counterfactual violations are answers that match the ground truth, and 26% of the counterfactual
mutants are refusals by the generating model.

We also propose a replacement relation, MR-Provenance-Swap, which gives both conflicting values a date
and swaps only the dates, so that the expected answer follows from the input. We evaluate it on real
Wikipedia revisions with three open models and report the pre-registered outcomes, including a
hypothesis that was not supported and a pilot that did not pass its gate.

with the authors of the audited framework on [date]. [Summarise any response.]
